How this calculator works

The calculator applies the Widmark method, combining alcohol consumed with your estimated body water and time elapsed to approximate current BAC.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a BAC calculator?

It gives an estimate only. Real BAC varies with food, medication, metabolism, and hydration — never use a calculator to decide whether it is safe to drive.

How long does alcohol stay in your system?

The body clears roughly one standard drink per hour on average, but this varies. Only time lowers BAC; coffee and water do not speed it up.

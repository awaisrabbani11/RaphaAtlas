# RaphaAtlas

> RaphaAtlas is a free health and fitness calculator hub. It provides accurate, single-purpose web tools — conception, body type, macronutrient, blood alcohol content, and related calculators — each paired with plain-language explanations of the underlying formulas and how to interpret results.

## Calculators

- [Pregnancy Conception Calculator](https://www.raphaatlas.com/conception-calculator): Estimates conception date, fertile window, gestational age, and due date from due date, last period, ultrasound, or IVF transfer.
- [Body Type Calculator](https://www.raphaatlas.com/body-type-calculator): Classifies body shape (hourglass, pear, rectangle, inverted triangle, and more) from bust, waist, and hip measurements.
- [Macro Calculator](https://www.raphaatlas.com/macro-calculator): Calculates daily protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets from BMR, activity level, and goal (weight loss, maintenance, muscle gain).
- [BAC Calculator](https://www.raphaatlas.com/bac-calculator): Estimates blood alcohol concentration using the Widmark formula, with time-to-sober output and jurisdiction-specific legal limits.

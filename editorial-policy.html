Editorial policy

This policy explains how we produce content and keep it trustworthy.

Evidence first

Articles are built from current, authoritative sources. We cite where claims come from and avoid presenting preliminary findings as settled fact.

Physician review

Health and medical articles are reviewed by an MBBS physician on our review board before publishing.

Calculators

Every calculator uses a published formula, verified against its source before release. We name the method used on each tool.

Corrections

We fix errors promptly. To report one, email the page URL and issue to our contact address.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages contain affiliate links, including Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Affiliate relationships never influence our clinical guidance.

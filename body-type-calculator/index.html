Frequently asked questions

What are the three body types?

The three somatotypes are ectomorph (lean, narrow frame), mesomorph (muscular, athletic build), and endomorph (softer, wider frame). Most people are a blend.

Can my body type change?

Your underlying frame is largely fixed, but body composition changes with training and nutrition, so your appearance and performance can shift substantially over time.

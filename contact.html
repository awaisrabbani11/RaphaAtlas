What we cannot help with

This is the part worth reading before you write, because it saves you waiting for an answer we are not able to give.

  • We cannot give personal medical advice. We do not know your history, your medications, your examination findings, or anything else a clinician would need. No amount of detail in an email changes that, and answering anyway would be irresponsible rather than helpful.
  • We cannot interpret your test results. A number without clinical context is not interpretable, which is precisely why your doctor asks about symptoms and history before commenting on a lab report. Our health hub explains how reference ranges work in general terms.
  • We cannot tell you whether a symptom is serious. If you are worried about a symptom, contact a clinician. If it is one of the emergency signs listed on our health page, contact emergency services now rather than emailing anyone.
  • We cannot recommend or adjust medication. Prescribing and dosing decisions belong to your prescriber.
If this is an emergency

Do not use this form. Contact your local emergency number or go to an emergency department. Chest pain, one-sided weakness or facial droop, difficulty breathing at rest, a sudden severe headache, or coughing blood all need immediate care rather than correspondence.

Reporting a correction

Accuracy reports are the most valuable messages we receive, and we would rather hear about a problem from you than leave it live. Our editorial policy sets out how corrections are handled: substantive changes — anything that alters meaning or could affect a reader's decision — are made to the page and noted on it, while minor typographic fixes are made without a note. If you are a clinician and something on the site misrepresents current guidance, please tell us and say so; we will treat it accordingly.

Privacy

We use what you send us to answer you and, where relevant, to fix the page in question. We do not sell your details or add you to a mailing list because you contacted us. Our privacy policy covers what is collected and why. Please do not send us medical records, test results, or other sensitive personal health information — we cannot act on it, and email is not a secure channel for it.

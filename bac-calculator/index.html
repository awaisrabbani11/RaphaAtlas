How this calculator works

The calculator applies the Widmark method, combining the alcohol consumed with your body water estimate and the time elapsed to approximate your current BAC.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a BAC calculator?

It gives an estimate only. Real BAC varies with food, medication, metabolism, and hydration, so never use a calculator to decide whether it is safe to drive.

How long does alcohol stay in your system?

The body clears roughly one standard drink per hour on average, but this varies by individual. Only time reduces BAC — coffee and water do not speed it up.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is an estimated conception date?

It is an estimate based on a typical cycle. Actual conception depends on when ovulation occurred, which varies, so an ultrasound gives a more precise date.

Can I find the exact day I conceived?

Rarely with certainty. Sperm can survive several days, so conception may occur days after intercourse. The tool gives a likely window, not an exact day.

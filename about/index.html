About RaphaAtlas

RaphaAtlas is an evidence-based health publication and free calculator library. We pair practical tools with clearly written articles on fitness, nutrition, and medicine — reviewed by practicing physicians before they publish.

Who we are

RaphaAtlas is a project of Growth Partners Global LLC. Our goal is simple: health information that informs rather than sells, and tools that show their working instead of hiding it.

How we work

Every calculator uses a published, checkable formula. Every clinical claim in our articles is checked against current evidence by an MBBS physician on our review board. Where the science is uncertain, we say so.

Editorial independence. We do not publish sponsored medical claims. Where pages contain affiliate links, they are disclosed and never change our clinical guidance.

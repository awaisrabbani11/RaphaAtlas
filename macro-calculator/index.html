How this calculator works

The calculator estimates your daily energy needs from your metrics and activity, then splits that total into protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets based on your goal.

Frequently asked questions

How are my macros calculated?

Your total daily calories are estimated first, then divided into grams of protein, carbohydrate, and fat using ratios suited to your goal and activity level.

Should I hit my macros exactly every day?

Aim for consistency across the week rather than perfection each day. Small daily variation is normal and does not undermine your results.

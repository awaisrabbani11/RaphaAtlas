Frequently asked questions

How are my macros calculated?

Your total daily calories are estimated first, then split into grams of protein, carbohydrate, and fat using ratios suited to your goal and activity level.

Should I hit my macros exactly every day?

Aim for consistency across the week rather than perfection each day. Small daily variation is normal and does not undermine results.
